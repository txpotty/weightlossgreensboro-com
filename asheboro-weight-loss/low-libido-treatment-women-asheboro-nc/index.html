    What Causes Low Libido in Asheboro

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Antidepressants, the pill, perimenopause, thyroid issues, stress, bad sleep, painful sex and relationship tension all show up often. Body image matters too. When low desire is persistent, distressing and not better explained by another cause, it may be diagnosed as hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. Women whose desire faded after a new medication, a baby, perimenopause or a hard year often benefit. You can start without a label. If lower desire does not bother you, there is nothing to fix, because distress is part of the definition. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in Asheboro

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. Bremelanotide, sold as Vyleesi and also called PT-141, is a 1.75 mg injection under the skin used at least 45 minutes before sex, no more than once a day or eight times a month. Oxytocin is compounded. It comes as a nasal spray or troche before intimacy, used off-label and without FDA approval. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Your provider may suggest changes to medications, sleep or treatment of vaginal dryness before or alongside any new prescription, since those fixes can help on their own. PT-141 can work with the first dose. Flibanserin builds over four to eight weeks, and providers usually stop it at eight weeks if nothing has improved. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in Asheboro

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Those effects are worse with alcohol or CYP3A4-blocking drugs, which is why the dose goes in at bedtime and the label limits drinking near that dose. Nausea is common with PT-141. It can also cause flushing, headache, injection site reactions, a brief rise in blood pressure and darker skin patches with repeated use. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. It is also avoided with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors such as fluconazole, clarithromycin or some HIV medications, and alcohol must be limited near bedtime. PT-141 has different limits. Women with high blood pressure that is not controlled, or with heart disease, should not use PT-141. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in Asheboro

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It tends to suit women who rarely drink and prefer one routine pill, though the average benefit in trials was modest, around one extra satisfying sexual event per month compared with placebo. PT-141 is taken only when wanted. Some women prefer that, though nausea can be a drawback. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
